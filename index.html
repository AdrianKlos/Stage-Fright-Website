3 Decades of Rock

Get ready to rock with Stage Fright!

For years, Stage Fright has been setting the Los Angeles music scene ablaze with their unique blend of rock. Night after night, this dynamic four-member band leaves audiences stunned by the sheer intensity and passion of their live performances. Known for playing in iconic venues in Los Angeles, New York, Chicago, Dallas, and more, Stage Fright is quickly gaining a reputation as one of the must-see acts in rock music today.

Their explosive live shows bring to life a collection of powerful songs like "Night Drive", "Heartbreaker", "Break the Chains", and "Lost in the Lights" — songs that have become instant crowd favorites with unforgettable hooks and driving riffs. Every note from the guitar, every beat from the drums, and every soaring vocal sends shockwaves through the crowd, making their concerts an unforgettable experience you can’t help but feel.

If you crave that authentic, electrifying rock ‘n’ roll spirit, Stage Fright delivers it with a force that keeps fans coming back for more.
